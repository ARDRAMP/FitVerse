Returns & Refunds Policy

We stand behind our gear. Enjoy 30 days of hassle-free returns on all eligible FitVerse products.

30-Day Window

Initiate returns within 30 days of item delivery.

Free Doorstep Pickup

Complimentary courier collection for all returnable items.

Fast Refund Processing

Refunds issued within 3-5 business days upon inspection.

How to Return an Item (4 Simple Steps)

1

Request Return

Log in to your profile or contact support with your Order ID.

2

Pack Item

Place item in original packaging with tags and manuals intact.

3

Courier Pickup

Our courier agent collects the package from your doorstep.

4

Receive Refund

Refund is credited to your original payment method or store credit.

Return Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a full refund or exchange, items must meet the following criteria:

  • Item must be unused, unwashed, and in the same original condition as received.
  • Original tags, protective plastic, and manufacturer box must be intact.
  • Heavy strength cages and machines must be un-assembled or disassembled into original boxes.
  • Non-Returnable Items: Sealed nutritional supplements that have been opened, customized apparel, and hygienic items (e.g. mouthguards).

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to process my refund?
Once your return package arrives at our warehouse, our quality control team inspects the item within 24 hours. Approved refunds are processed back to your UPI, credit card, or bank account in 3 to 5 business days.
What if I received a damaged or defective item?
If your shipment arrives damaged or defective, please notify us within 48 hours with photo/video proof. We will send an immediate replacement at zero cost and arrange express pickup.
Can I exchange for a different size or product?
Yes! Exchanges for size differences (apparel, belts, wrist wraps) are completely free. Select "Exchange" when submitting your return request.

Need Help with a Return?

Our customer care team is available 24/7 to assist you with returns, exchanges, and warranty claims.
