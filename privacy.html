Privacy Policy

At FitVerse, we value your trust. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, protect, and use your personal information across our website and services.

1. Overview & Data Protection Principles

FitVerse ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of all athletes, customers, and visitors who interact with our e-commerce platform. This policy applies to all personal information collected through fitverse.com and associated subdomains.

Core Guarantee: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you directly provide to us, as well as data gathered automatically when you navigate through our store.

Category Examples Purpose
Account & Contact Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Password Account creation, sign-in authentication, order confirmation
Order & Fulfillment Shipping Address, Billing Details, Purchase History Order processing, delivery, invoice generation
Payment Info Encrypted Tokenized Payment Tokens (PCI-DSS) Payment execution via secure gateways (Stripe/Razorpay)
Technical Data IP Address, Device ID, Browser Type, Session Logs Platform analytics, security, fraud prevention

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used to deliver an exceptional e-commerce and equipment fulfillment experience. Specifically, we process data to:

  • Process, fulfill, ship, and track your fitness equipment orders.
  • Provide personalized product recommendations based on your browsing history and wishlist.
  • Communicate critical updates regarding delivery schedules, warranty registrations, and safety notices.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions or unauthorized account activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal, tax, and regulatory compliance obligations.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies to maintain your shopping cart, session authentication state, and user preferences. You can control cookie preferences in your browser settings.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for cart functionality, authentication, and secure checkout.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us measure site traffic, page speed, and navigation trends to improve user experience.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember currency settings, wishlist items, and dark mode toggles.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

We share minimal necessary data only with trusted operational partners required to run our business:

  • Logistics & Freight Carriers: Name, shipping address, and phone number shared with courier partners (FedEx, BlueDart, DHL) for delivery.
  • Payment Gateways: Encrypted transaction data processed by PCI-compliant payment processors.
  • Infrastructure & Hosting: Encrypted cloud storage and secure server hosting environments.

6. Data Retention & Security Measures

We implement industry-leading technical and organizational security controls to protect your data:

  • All web traffic is encrypted using 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3/SSL).
  • Passwords and sensitive tokens are hashed using salted cryptographic algorithms.
  • Data is stored in high-security data centers with restricted access controls and continuous vulnerability monitoring.

7. Your Legal Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you hold the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal account and associated data.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from promotional email communications at any time.

If you have questions, feedback, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to our dedicated Data Protection Office:
