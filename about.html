OUR HISTORY

FROM WORKSHOP TO GLOBAL EMPIRE

2020

The Genesis in Bangalore

FitVerse was launched with a single product line: Olympic power barbells engineered with custom knurling patterns that didn't tear up hands during heavy pulls.

2022

Biomechanical Innovation Facility

Opened our dedicated R&D testing facility. Introduced laser-cut 11-gauge power racks, calibrated steel plates, and high-density foam utility benches.

2024

Commercial Gym & Bundle Suites

Expanded into complete custom equipment suites, air fan conditioning bikes, dual cable crossovers, and commercial gym outfitting across Asia.

2026

50,000+ Athletes Worldwide

Now powering over 50,000 home gyms, powerlifting clubs, and commercial studios with an average 4.9/5 satisfaction rating.
